Our Story

It all began on a sunny day at the beach, much like the one where we'll say "I do". Amanda was reading her favorite book under a palm tree when Ryan, quite literally, stumbled upon her while chasing after an escaped volleyball. Coffee stains, laughter, and a shared love for ocean sunsets followed that fateful encounter.

Three years later, on the anniversary of that first meeting, Ryan got down on one knee in the exact same spot where they first met, waves crashing in the background, presenting a ring held inside a seashell.
